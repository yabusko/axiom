Click-through flow (the 3-minute demo scenario)

Start from an already-built field, go straight to a gap, draft a direction, shortlist it — the scenario this product is built to nail.

  1. 1
    Start — reopen the saved "retrieval-augmented generation" field
  2. 2
    Landscape — see the topics, rising concepts, and key papers
  3. 3
    Research gaps — list — scan the 15 ranked candidate gaps
  4. 4
    Research gaps — detail — read the top gap's bridge, evidence, and cross-check
  5. 5
    Paper panel open — open an evidence paper from the gap detail screen
  6. 6
    Shortlist — the drafted direction, with notes and a paper to read
  7. 7
    Markdown export — the copy-pasteable summary for a supervisor meeting
